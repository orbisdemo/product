Frequently asked questions

Get to know Orbis.

A clearer view of the platform, its use cases and where it fits in your enterprise ecosystem.

What is Orbis?

Orbis is an enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform designed to connect business demand, portfolio planning, vendor commitments, project execution and engineering delivery in one decision lifecycle.

How does Orbis relate to IT Financial Management?

Orbis connects investment evaluation and planning with budgets, commitments and available project performance information to support financial visibility and accountability.

Can Orbis support FinOps and cloud investment decisions?

Orbis can connect cloud-related initiatives with business priorities, investment plans and delivery context. Detailed billing analytics, resource optimization and automated cost allocation depend on supported product capabilities and integrations.

How does Orbis support AI economics?

Orbis can place AI initiatives within the wider investment lifecycle, connecting business cases, planned expenditure, risks, approvals and delivery outcomes. Model-level or token-level cost analytics should be confirmed against available capabilities.

Does Orbis replace existing enterprise applications?

Orbis is positioned as a connected decision layer that complements existing enterprise applications. Supported integrations and connection methods should be confirmed for each system.

Who is Orbis designed for?

Orbis is relevant to CIOs, CTOs, CFOs, IT finance teams, FinOps leaders, portfolio and PMO leaders, procurement teams, AI leaders and engineering stakeholders.
